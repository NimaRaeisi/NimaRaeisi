### Hi there 👋
💫 About Me:
As a PhD student in Civil Engineering, specializing in geotechnical and hydrogeological engineering at the University of Calgary, I am driven by a relentless curiosity to understand the intricate dynamics of groundwater–permafrost systems and their interaction with the environment. My academic journey — spanning geology, hydrogeology, machine learning, and advanced numerical modeling — has equipped me to tackle complex hydrogeological and geotechnical challenges in cold-regions environments.

Experience:

🔬 Permafrost-Groundwater Research: Investigating the hydrogeological and ecological impacts of watercourse diversion in dynamic permafrost environments, as part of a research collaboration between the University of Calgary and WSP (via Mitacs) supporting the Giant Mine Remediation Project in Yellowknife, NT.

🧊 Geophysics & Site Characterization: Applying ERT and seismic geophysical methods to characterize permafrost-groundwater systems and build conceptual site models.

🤖 Machine Learning for Groundwater Forecasting: During my MSc, applied machine learning methods to forecast groundwater levels using qualitative and hydrological data — leveraging Scikit-learn to uncover predictive relationships between water quality parameters and groundwater quantity.

📈 Data Analysis and Visualization: Harnessing Python (Scikit-learn, Pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn) to analyze and visualize hydrogeological data, uncovering insights to inform decision-making.

💻 Numerical Modeling: Proficient in industry-standard groundwater modeling software including MODFLOW and GMS, used to simulate groundwater flow, contaminant transport, and coupled thermal-hydrological processes under climate and permafrost change.
